`taxonomy.json` covers eight structural relationships, 22 active techniques, 20 active likelihood classes and passive MemberOf.
`profile.json` gives each active class an ordinal work rubric and its own conditional success question/rationale.
`source-a-inventory.json` defines bounded identities/lineages before search.

Probability follows full ordered active history; passive transitions contribute no factor.
C=0 work still has a q event; WinRM preparation is included once rather than added twice.
I binds typed technical consequence at the target, never severity of an edge.

New operational bounds are explicit AI-assisted expert scenario judgments of residual whole-workflow failure allowances.
Their endpoints are proposals for B to assess, not numbers derived from Microsoft/BloodHound documentation, C/I or observed scenario success.
Historical reset/activation/elevated WinRM values retain their provenance but their broader history equivalence is pending.
Offline AS-REP and TGS recovery remain unrated until compatible compute coverage and conditional password-rank basis exist.
No arbitrary uniform password model is transferred into production.

The binding compiler only expands a finite declared rating history domain into native exact records.
It does not evaluate graph state, discover paths, rank candidates or replace an engine/provider.
Every record names its full ordered prefix, actor/source/target/auth/resource, method, scenario, snapshot, budget, retry policy and horizon.
The stable/no-depletion history equivalence is an additional conditional scenario assumption requiring B acceptance.
No wildcard marginal q or independence assumption is used.
Expansion over the declared record cap fails closed, rather than silently truncating rating records.
Unlisted identities, repeated work, changed lineage or secret invalidation are outside this domain.

The existing public automatic resolver consumes the resulting pending native envelopes and refuses their numbers.
A disposable synthetic derivative checks wiring/arithmetic and cannot establish production adoption or raw numerical coverage.
Coverage reports retain all generated candidates and publish accepted, test-only potential, broader all-technique, truncation and missing-basis counts separately.
The 80% target is an acceptance target, never assumed achieved.

B handoff: review each class endpoint/rationale and finite history-domain digest, adopt only defensible groups with exact references, consume native registries through existing public resolution, and retain `estimated_expert_scenario` plus actual adoption status in public output.
Implementing assumption-profile ingestion, general source/target contracts, batch source prioritization, search/UI and remediation counterfactuals remains B-owned.
Group initial compromise must not be represented as login credentials for a Group.
