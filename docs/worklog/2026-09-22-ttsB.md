## Điều học được / quyết định

- Reconciliation is the single representation-level merge authority; canonical identity
  resolution and normalized-identity uniqueness remain owned by the normalizer stage.
- `record_id` identifies the logical object, while `record_locator` identifies one
  immutable occurrence in a JSON envelope.
- Bare namespace-relative local SIDs are not merged without stable scope evidence.
- Unknown list fields and conflicting scalar values remain unresolved instead of gaining
  an unauthoritative deterministic winner.
- Raw identifier alias handling is shared by loader, reconciliation, and normalization;
  display names, DNs, and labels remain invalid identity fallbacks.
- Item-level relationship lineage prevents downstream graph construction from attaching
  the first occurrence or every occurrence to evidence it did not supply.

- #94 evidence phải giữ ranh giới `Structural Relationship != Attack Technique / transition != Observed Activity`; upstream labels và EventID đơn lẻ không phải ground truth.
